A Sanctuary Above the City

A private highland estate for intimate celebrations, weddings and corporate retreats.

Nestled in the cool, misty hills of Genting Highlands, Dani Hill is an exclusive private estate designed for intimate weddings, corporate retreats, and bespoke celebrations. Surrounded by lush greenery and serene highland landscapes, the estate offers a rare escape just a short drive from Kuala Lumpur, where nature, elegance, and privacy come together. Formerly known as Puncak Dani, the estate has been refurbished and renamed Dani Hill.

Important Information

Booking & Payment

  • A 50% deposit secures your booking.
  • The remaining 50% is payable 30 days before the event date.
  • Bookings are confirmed on receipt of the deposit.
  • The deposit is non-refundable, non-amendable and non-transferable.

Event Guidelines

  • Standard capacity of up to 300 pax, subject to event layout.
  • Events of 301 to 500 pax carry a RM3,000 surcharge.
  • Setup, the event and teardown must all be completed within the booked package hours.
  • Overtime charges apply, subject to availability.

Usage Policy

  • No smoking indoors.
  • No cooking or open flames.
  • Only pre-cooked or catered food is permitted.

Arrival & Departure

  • Check-in and check-out times follow your booked package.
  • Overnight stays accommodate up to 30 pax: 15 at The Mansion and 15 at Villa Dani.
  • Guests are asked to observe all house rules.

Security Deposit

  • A RM3,000 security deposit is required.
  • Refunded within 14 working days after the post-event inspection.
  • Damage, losses or cleaning surcharges are deducted from it.

Additional Notes

  • The client is responsible for any damage or loss of property during the event.
  • Management may enforce venue policies to keep the event safe and pleasant.
  • Management may cancel a booking if full payment is not received on time.
  • Management is not liable for loss of or damage to personal belongings.
